A People's Bar

Lilli von Kohl is a modern gathering place — a people's bar where everyone feels welcome, whether it's for an after-work beer, a casual wine evening, or a Friday night with live music and DJs.

Here, neighbors, colleagues, and culture lovers come together in an atmosphere that balances local authenticity with an international vibe.

Lilli is the place where you can experience the true Copenhagen spirit, presented in a way that's as fresh as the neighborhood around us.

Lilli von Kohl

The Story Behind Lilli von Kohl

As we wish to celebrate our location in the midst of Carlsberg's long and remarkable history, the name Lilli von Kohl is also drawn from that legacy.

Lilli was a modern young woman who, among other things, wrote film screenplays and was part of Copenhagen's cultural life at the time.
In the early 1900s, she married the somewhat older Carl Jacobsen, though the marriage lasted only a few years.
Without knowing the full details of Lilli von Kohl's story, we have a clear impression of her as a vibrant and uncomplicated person who brought out the bon vivant in Carl.
The rose in our logo is taken from this very period, when it is said that Carl would entertain at his own frequent parties with a rose held between his teeth.
